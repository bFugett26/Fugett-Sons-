A small crew with a big commitment to quality

Fugett & Son Painting and Drywall is a father and son owned business based in Burlington County, operated by the son alongside a small, proficient crew. Our business is built on a simple commitment: put our best effort into every job and give our customers results we can proudly stand behind.

We believe great service is about more than the finished surface. It is about listening to your needs, communicating clearly, respecting your space, and paying attention to the details along the way. From careful preparation to the finishing touches, we approach each painting and drywall project with care and a commitment to getting it right.

Our crew may be small, but that is part of what makes our approach personal. We work closely together and stay focused on the quality of our work, giving your project the attention it deserves. Whether you want to refresh a room, repair damaged drywall, or bring a new vision to your space, our goal is to make the experience straightforward and the results something you can enjoy.

At Fugett & Son Painting and Drywall, we know you are trusting us with more than walls. You are trusting us with your home or business. We work hard to honor that trust through dependable service, thoughtful workmanship, and pride in every project we take on.
